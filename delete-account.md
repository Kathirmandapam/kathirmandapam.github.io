# Delete your Kathirmandapam account and data

You can delete your account at any time, whether or not your plan is active.

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open **My profile**.
2. Choose **Delete account** and confirm.

Your profile, photos and sign-in details are deleted right away.

## If you cannot use the app
If you have lost your phone or removed the app, ask us to delete your account instead:
- **WhatsApp:** +91 89710 04506
- **Email:** kathirmandapam.in@gmail.com

Send the mobile number registered on the profile. We will call that number back to confirm it is you before deleting, and we reply within 7 days.

## What is deleted
- Your profile details, partner preferences and photos.
- Your sign-in device key and sessions.
- Your shortlists, interests and insight counts tied to your profile.

## What is kept, and for how long
- Copies in backups are removed within 35 days.
- Records of reports, blocks and administrator actions may be kept for up to 3 years where needed to protect other members or meet a legal duty.
- Daily view and interest totals without names are kept for 90 days.
