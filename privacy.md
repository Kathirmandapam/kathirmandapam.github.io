# Privacy Policy for Kathirmandapam

**Effective from** the first public release of the app.

Kathirmandapam is a community matrimonial app for Ambalavasi and Nair families of Kerala. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

**Who is responsible:** Rajasekhara Warrier, an individual, Bangalore, Karnataka, India.

## 1. What we collect
**You give us, when you create a profile**
- Your name, and the name of the person managing the profile if it is not you; mobile number, and optionally an alternate or WhatsApp number and email.
- Gender, date of birth, height, marital status, mother tongue, a short "about me" text, diet, smoking and drinking habits.
- Community and sub-community (self-declared), family name, native district and native places, nakshatram, and optionally rashi and dosha jathakam. We never ask for a horoscope chart or upload.
- Education, occupation, employer, and optionally annual income.
- Where you live now, residency status and willingness to relocate.
- Family details (parents, siblings) and your partner preferences. Most of these are optional.
- Up to four photos.

We do **not** collect identity documents, place or time of birth, or health details.

**Collected automatically**
- A cryptographic key created on your phone to sign you in (we store only the public half), and a sign-in history for your account.
- Basic technical data such as IP address and app version, used for security and to limit abuse.
- A record of profile views, shortlists and interests, so we can show you counts of how many members saw or showed interest in your profile. We never show you who they were.
- We do not use crash reporting or push notifications today. We will update this policy before turning either on.

## 2. Why we use it
- To run your profile and let approved members find each other.
- For an admin to review each profile before it is shown, and to confirm by a call or message that the number belongs to the person who made the profile.
- To handle interests, contact sharing, reports and blocks.
- To keep the service safe: stopping fake profiles and abuse, and following the law.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell or rent it to anyone. If that ever changes, we will tell members first, and ads will never use profile data.

## 3. Who can see what
- **Only approved members** can see profiles, and members see profiles of the opposite gender.
- **Your phone number and the name of the person managing the profile stay hidden until an interest is accepted.** After acceptance, the other side can see them for a limited time, which is currently 10 days, unless you both confirm you have connected.
- You may choose to hide your photos and some other details (such as income, employer, family details and native places) until an interest is accepted.
- Our admins can see profiles, photos and contact details to review and moderate them.
- We do not give your data to anyone else, except our service providers in section 5, or where the law requires it.
- **Screenshots** are blocked inside the app. This cannot stop someone photographing a screen, so share only what you are comfortable with.

## 4. Consent
You consent when you create a profile and accept these terms. Profiles made on someone's behalf should only be made with that person's consent. A profile made without the owner's consent is removed on investigation. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

## 5. Where data is stored and who helps us run the app
- Your data is stored on Google Cloud Platform infrastructure in India (Mumbai), in a managed database and private cloud storage for photos. Photos are resized and stripped of location and camera information before storage, and are shown only through short-lived links.
- Data is encrypted in transit.
- These providers act only on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes.

## 6. How long we keep it
- While your account is active: your profile and photos.
- If your plan expires, your profile is hidden and locked, but kept, so you can renew. An expired profile is deleted after 12 months if it is not renewed.
- After you delete your account: your profile, photos and sign-in details are deleted immediately from the live system. Copies in backups are removed within 35 days.
- Safety records (reports, blocks and admin action logs) may be kept for up to 3 years where needed to protect other members or meet a legal duty.
- Daily view and interest totals, without names, are kept for 90 days.

## 7. Your rights
- **See and correct:** view and edit your profile in the app at any time. Some verified details are locked after approval; ask the admin if one needs a correction.
- **Delete:** delete your account and data from within the app, whether or not your plan is active.
- **Withdraw consent, and complain:** contact us using the details below. We reply within 7 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
- You can report or block any member whatever your plan.

## 8. Children
Kathirmandapam is only for people of legal marriage age in India (18 or over for women, 21 or over for men). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger, and we delete such a profile when we find it.

## 9. Security
We use device-bound sign-in, encrypted connections, private storage with short-lived links, access limits for staff, and logs of administrator actions. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 10. Changes
If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies and update the effective date above.
